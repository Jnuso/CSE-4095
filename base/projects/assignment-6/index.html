ASSIGNMENT 06 / 07

Chopsticks AI.

Tap, split, and plan ahead in a browser version of Chopsticks with an opponent that reasons through the game tree.

Game AIMinimaxDynamic programming
01 / THE GAME

Play against the search

Choose to play as either side against the computer, share the screen with a second player, or watch AI play against AI. The default game uses the assignment’s 20-move limit.

The computer uses minimax values built with a dynamic-programming table. Its analysis panel shows the best move and lets you explore possible next positions. An optional strength control changes how often the human-facing opponent departs from its best choice.

02 / WHAT TO TRY

Look beneath each move

  • Play as Player A and inspect the recommended move before choosing yours.
  • Switch to AI vs AI and step through the match.
  • Change the move limit to see how the opening evaluation changes.
