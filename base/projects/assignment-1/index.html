Recursive Checker Swap.

A small board makes recursion visible. Follow every adjacent swap as two groups of checkers move into alternating positions.

RecursionAlgorithmsInteractive visualization
01 / THE IDEA

Make the hidden steps visible

The visualizer turns a recursive checker-swapping solution into a sequence you can watch and inspect. Choose how many checkers to start with, then play the animation or advance one move at a time.

Alongside the board, the page shows the current arrangement, the target pattern, the active recursion level, and a history of swaps.

Launch the demo ↗
02 / WHAT TO TRY

Change the size of the problem

  • Start with four checkers per color and step through the first few moves.
  • Increase the checker count to see how the recursive pattern scales.
  • Use the history panel to connect each board change with its swap.
